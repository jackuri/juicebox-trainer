Cheats for your PC games.
Make your own, with or without AI.

Everything in one free app

🎮

Cheats while you play

Switches, sliders and buttons for each cheat, your own keys, controller combos, presets, a see-through overlay and a phone remote. Play with cheats starts the game and turns them on for you.

🔎

Make a cheat yourself

Find a value asks what you see in the game ("you have 1,250 gold") and finds it for you. No number on screen, like a health bar? It works with "it went down" too.

🤖

An AI helper

Tell it what you want, in your own words. It tries things in the game with you, keeps only cheats that work, and can fix a cheat while you keep playing.

⚡

Instant cheats for Unreal games

One click reads many Unreal Engine games and makes cheats straight away: game speed, move speed, jump height, infinite health or ammo.

🌐

Web games too

Games that run in a web page open in JuiceBox Trainer's own window: find a number, lock it, give it a key, or combine every enemy's health into one cheat.

💾

Save backups and editing

Your saves are backed up when you play. Restore one in a click, see what changed, and edit values in many save formats.

📄

Cheat Engine tables

Turn a .CT table into simple switches. JuiceBox Trainer reads its scripts but never runs them, and keeps the maker's name and page with it.

🏆

A community that levels up

Share your packs, rate others' and check in each day for XP. Climb from Recruit Packer all the way to Pack God on the leaderboard.

How it works

  1. 1

    Download and open it

    Install it for your Windows account. No admin rights and no sign-up.

  2. 2

    Pick a game

    Your installed games are listed with their covers. Click Play with cheats.

  3. 3

    Cheat, or make cheats

    Use a pack, find a value yourself, or ask the AI. Everything is undone when you close the game.

Fair play, built in

JuiceBox Trainer is for single-player games, co-op with friends, and online games without anti-cheat. It checks every game first and refuses to work while a game's anti-cheat runs: that can't be switched off. Please don't spoil other players' fun in public or ranked matches.
